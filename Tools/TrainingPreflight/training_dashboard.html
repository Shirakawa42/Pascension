Training progress

Loss, KL, clipping and gradient summaries include attempted minibatches, including rejected attempts. Value loss is the clipped training objective, not held-out prediction error. Entropy includes forced actions; archive scores compare changing opponents. Use frozen evaluations to assess strength.

Latest learner action mix

Counts cover stored learner decisions in the latest completed generation. Select, finish, page, and integer are staged wrapper choices; they are not separate engine submissions. This is a behavior diagnostic, not a card-strength estimate.

Hardware and process health

GPU readings cover the whole device, including other applications. Driver VRAM in WSL can differ from CUDA free memory. CPU and RAM cover the Linux/WSL guest. Utilization is a bottleneck signal, not a measure of policy strength.
