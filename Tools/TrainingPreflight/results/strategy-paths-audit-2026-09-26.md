# Strategy-path audit — 26 September 2026

This is a read-only audit of the current all-DLC Duel rules, V7 policy/observation contract, and existing frozen probes. It does not run games, update weights, rebuild a host, or alter training. The quantitative panel below is **generation 7272**, not the live generation: [640-game early-V7 probe](v7-pilot-choice-coverage.json). It contains 246,798 sampled decisions, zero censors, no optimizer updates and no CUDA use. The parent investigation supplies newer measurements separately.

## Conclusion

The evidence supports broad **availability** coverage, not comprehensive **strategic competence**. Every active relic, destiny and market definition had acquisition observations in the earlier pooled panel; many apparently neglected market cards are used frequently through fast-play. However, aggregate acquisitions and even exhaust counts leave major questions unanswered: activation timing, threshold sequencing, harmful modes, selective retention, board protection, information use and several combinatorial engines. Low scores cannot establish that these paths are intrinsically weak. [Earlier acquisition panel and scope](choice-coverage-investigation-2026-09-26.md), [actual legal-action generation](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L1790).

The strongest specific unresolved signals are:

1. **Reactor Drone's two modes remain almost exactly equiprobable on average** in the early-V7 probe: 137 menus, mean probabilities 0.500286918 and 0.499713080; selections 71 and 66. Unlike Volos, it has no categorical mode head. When its effect is copied by an effect whose source is not Reactor Drone, the three-gem choice does not banish the copier; this gives a particularly useful controlled competence case. Near-uniform aggregate probability is suspicious, not proof of state-insensitive play. [Probe, `choice:127,154,193,128:0/1`](v7-pilot-choice-coverage.json), [Reactor mode and source safeguard](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Content/ShardsDuelSet.cs#L520), [V7's Volos-only residual](../experiments/choice_policy_v7.py#L66).
2. **Conditional exhausts may be used before their condition is met.** The legal menu checks exhaustion and affordability, not whether the conditional effect currently produces anything. Marking an exhaust as “used” therefore does not establish useful execution. This is legal game behavior and an unmeasured policy-quality hypothesis, not a reproduced engine defect. [Legal menu](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L1824), [activation consumes the exhaust before executing the effect](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L715).
3. **Shard Defiant's existing probe key merges Keep and Banish for the same revealed card.** Both modes carry the same card ID; the probe records `choice:context:card`, losing the ordinal. Those counts cannot establish that both modes are understood. This is a limitation of the independent coverage probe, not a demonstrated training or dashboard bug. [Probe key](../experiments/choice_coverage_probe.py#L61), [two modes](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Content/ShardsHorizonSet.cs#L668).

## Exact gates suitable for inexpensive observation-side diagnostics

For the current raw schema: own health=`obs[16]*50`, mastery=`obs[17]*30`, gems=`obs[18]*20`, power=`obs[19]*100`, champions=`obs[45]*20`; faction plays H/U/O/W/A=`obs[49:54]*20`; corresponding ally plays=`obs[56:61]*20`. Use rounding consistent with the encoder. [Encoder](../experiments/HostV6/Encoder.cs#L89), [enum order](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsTypes.cs#L21).

- `datic_secrets_duel`: Order ally plays>=2.
- `paradigm_shift_duel`: Order>0 AND Wraethe>0.
- `forged_in_flame`: Wraethe>0 AND Homodeus>0; realized benefit additionally needs a banish target.
- `biotech_enhancements`: Homodeus>0 AND Undergrowth>0.
- `crystal_gate`: Order>0 AND Undergrowth>0; realized benefit additionally needs an eligible row card.
- `true_leader`: any of the five faction-play counts>=3.
- `synthesis`: mastery>=15; `stolen_futures`: mastery>=10.
- `primus_pilus_duel`: champions>=3; `war_bound`: champions>=2.
- `strategic_mastermind`: health>=40.

Count “exhaust selected while gate false” separately from “missed productive opportunity”: a useless free exhaust at the end of a turn with no way to enable it may be neutral. An immediate HP delta of zero does not establish useless healing when Talons/Nectar is active. Neither does zero immediate resource gain establish a wasted activation for Praetorian02 (persistent shield doubling), RuBoVai (shield ignore), NumeriDrones (future deployment), or GeneralDecurion (past/future copying). ## Representation and curriculum limits

- V7 adds categorical scores only for Volos, and 6%/2% mixture mass only for legal normal relic/destiny choices. It does not intentionally randomize market acquisitions, sequence plans, copy targets, optional retention or Reactor/Defiant modes. Random hero setup broadens hero exposure but does not guarantee exploring each hero's specialized deck plan. [V7 policy](../experiments/choice_policy_v7.py#L1).
- Reactor modes differ primarily by ordinal/128 and lack cost/effect semantics; Defiant's modes share the revealed card embedding, which cancels in their score difference. The nonlinear numeric branch and observation/title features can still distinguish them, so this is a capacity/learning-efficiency concern rather than mathematical unrepresentability. [Candidate encoding](../experiments/HostV6/Encoder.cs#L253), [candidate scorer](../model.py#L55).
- The state/value trunk does not aggregate current candidates. Revealed candidates are compared independently against the shared state query, and no recurrent policy state is used. The host preserves a limited owner-known center top, not all legal historical knowledge. This especially limits card-copy/menu comparison, opponent deck inference and longer market-control planning. [Policy](../model.py#L35), [knowledge contract](../experiments/HostV6/README.md).
- Exact public entities are truncated after24; aggregate counts and legal candidates remain available. PlayedThisTurn is represented indirectly through current zones and faction counters rather than an explicit complete ordered card history. Effects depending on precise played-card categories after removals can therefore be harder to infer. This source limitation has not been measured as a frequent live failure. [Entity truncation and zone channels](../experiments/HostV6/Encoder.cs#L29).
- The prior large-menu test establishes reachability but the early natural probe encountered no page actions. Later-page competence remains untested. [Adapter coverage evidence](choice-coverage-source-audit-2026-09-26.md).

## Prioritized follow-up

1. Run a fresh frozen balanced probe recording conditional success/no-effect exhausts, threshold ordering, mode ordinals, repeat/retention decisions and contextual alternatives. Keep diagnostic games separate from training/balance associations.
2. Use controlled **one-decision paired scenarios** for copied Reactor's free extra gem, M29 Infinity, M19 UnknownGod, Talons-at-cap healing, six-gem lethal WhateverItTakes, and lethal tutor/Comet. These can expose actual policy errors without guessing from marginal rankings.
3. For rare strategic engines, use legal paired continuations with matched hero/seat/deck-state distributions and natural opponents. Assignment atM10 tests continuation, not prior deck construction; a prospective announced strategy curriculum is a different intervention.
4. Improve representation only after a reproducible decision failure. Any training change must use a new pinned identity and exact behavior probabilities; do not hot-edit the live policy or impose external actions while pretending PPO sampled the unchanged policy.

No claim here establishes that an action is universally bad or that every strategy has been mastered. The evidence separates observed availability, recorded use, plausible opportunity cost, and competence that still requires conditional measurement.

## Follow-up: damage-allocation inference and taunt normalization

This source-only follow-up supports a new frozen diagnostic; it adds no game batch.

The engine places living **player targets first**, then attackable champion targets. Each champion option announces `Amount = max(1, effective defense − current damage)` and marks taunt with `Required=true`. Per-card attack vetoes exclude targets outright. In 1v1 with a directly assignable opponent, `Min=Max=power`: all power must be assigned. With the sole opponent behind taunt, `Min=0`, allowing waste. [Split construction](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L1065).

The adapter first chooses a player allocation through interval subdivision, then the champion allocation. Its final target is forced to consume the remainder only when the request's minimum requires it. Therefore **infer champion allocation as `power − player allocation` only if** the request has exactly two enabled options, exactly one opposing champion, and `Min=Max>0`. Merely observing one opposing champion is insufficient: behind taunt, the final champion allocation remains independently optional. To measure taunt properly, track the second target's final leaf or the submitted answer. [Adapter final-target minimum](../experiments/HostV6/Adapter.cs#L201).

For that sole-champion, mandatory-full-allocation subset, assigning a positive amount smaller than its announced remaining defense cannot destroy it. Damage marks have no persistent benefit: they are cleared on every champion at cleanup, before the next turn. A nonlethal hit only records marks and emits `ShardsChampionDamagedEvent`; no current card effect subscribes to that event. With exactly one enemy champion there is no earlier destruction of another champion's defense aura to lower the threshold. [Damage application](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L2033), [all-player mark reset](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L1515), [effective-defense auras](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L982).

Call this **sublethal power diverted from face**, rather than a guaranteed lost win. Additional face damage may be completely shielded, so the alternatives can tie in game-state outcome. Testudo shields can only reduce the champion hit; they cannot turn a sub-defense assignment into a kill. A differing shield prompt/response may reveal information or produce different behavior from a fallible opponent. Comparisons that assume identical defensive responses should state that assumption. Effective defense must come from the actual announced split option (or its encoder reconstruction), not printed defense; it includes defense auras. [Authorized defense encoding](../experiments/HostV6/Encoder.cs#L229), [Testudo and shield resolution](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L1220).

Taunt is **not enforced by rejecting the submitted answer**. Generic validation checks counts and enabled known IDs; it does not enforce the `Required` marker. The engine then discards assignments to a taunt-protected player and their other champions unless the taunt receives lethal announced damage. With Testudo present, a preliminary lethal taunt allocation can subsequently be reduced by shields; if taunt survives, the other champion hits and face damage resolve as zero. These legal-but-wasted assignments are an important competence metric and must not be counted as host rejection bugs. [Answer validation](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L390), [initial taunt normalization](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L1168), [post-shield taunt gate](../../../Assets/Scripts/Shards/Engine/ShardsEngine.cs#L1220).
